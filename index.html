09 — questions & answers everything you need to know before starting

project
inquiries.

Transparent details on turnaround times, AI integration, deliverables, and how we collaborate.

Have a custom brief? Send an inquiry directly ↗

Turnaround depends on project scope. A standard 30–60 second motion graphics spot or commercial edit typically takes 4 to 8 business days from script approval. Generative AI explorations and styleframes can be delivered in 48 hours for rapid alignment.

AI tools (Google Veo 3, Midjourney, ComfyUI) are used to invent complex worlds, stylized textures, and visual metaphors that would otherwise require massive Hollywood budgets. Once generated, every shot is graded, retimed, composited in After Effects, and unified with hand-crafted typography and sound design.

Yes. Motion without sound loses half its impact. Every project includes tailored foley sound effects, riser whooshes, atmospheric sub-bass, neural voiceover casting (or human VO sync), and broadcast-standard master level balancing (-14 LUFS).

All projects include 2 to 3 structured revision rounds via Frame.io or timestamped reviews. Final deliverables are packaged in 4K ProRes 422, uncompressed H.264/H.265 MP4s, and formatted for horizontal broadcast (16:9), vertical social (9:16), or square feeds (1:1).
